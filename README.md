## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Sales and Profit:** correlation ≈ `0.997`
  - Very strong positive relationship.

- **Sales and Quantity:** correlation ≈ `0.723`
  - Strong positive relationship.

- **Sales and Unit Price:** correlation ≈ `0.578`
  - Moderate positive relationship.

- **Discount and Sales:** correlation ≈ `-0.009`
  - Almost no linear relationship.

- After cleaning, the dataset contains **60 rows and 15 columns** with no missing values or duplicate records.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Encourage larger order quantities**
   - Quantity has a strong positive correlation with Sales.
   - Product bundles, combo offers, and quantity-based promotions may help increase order value.

2. **Do not depend heavily on discounts**
   - Discount showed almost no linear relationship with Sales in this dataset.
   - The business should evaluate discount campaigns carefully rather than relying on discounts as the main sales strategy.
